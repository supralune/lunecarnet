A durable note usually answers three questions: what problem was being solved, why this approach was chosen, and how you know it worked.

## Record the Judgment, Not Only the Steps

Procedures become outdated as tools change, but decision criteria transfer more easily. Record the constraints, alternatives, and reasons you rejected them so your future self can reuse the experience.

## Attach a Verification Method

A conclusion that cannot be checked is difficult to trust. Even a single command, screenshot location, or expected result can make a note substantially more useful.
