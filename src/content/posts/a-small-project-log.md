The greatest risk in a small project is often not technical difficulty, but allowing the scope to expand before anything is delivered.

## Define the Smallest Complete Experience

Small does not mean careless. The first version should let someone complete one whole task and see a clear result. Everything else can grow around real feedback.

## Treat Documentation as Part of the Product

Clear setup instructions, example data, and obvious customization points reduce the cost of understanding a project. For a template, the documentation is one of the most important interfaces.
