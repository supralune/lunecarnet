A good personal website does not need to speak loudly. It can feel more like a well-kept desk: important material is within reach, while secondary details remain quietly at the edge.

## Start with the Content

This template begins with its two most important forms of content—personal notes and academic work—and then gives each a clear hierarchy. Decoration supports reading instead of filling empty space.

## One Shared Visual Language

The blog and academic variants share the same paper tone, blue accent, fine rules, and serif headings. Their difference comes mainly from rhythm: the blog feels editorial, while the academic site behaves more like a careful index.

> Consistency does not require every page to look identical. It asks each page to speak the same visual language.

## Leave Room for the Author

Every example can be replaced directly. You can also change which variant appears at the root URL or hide the template switcher in the header.
